# Claude Friend

A friend called Claude who follows me around, talks out loud in a deep voice,
and does what I ask. Type "claude" in chat and he shows up next to you.

Things you can ask him:

- follow me, stay, come here
- make it day, make it night
- make it rain, make it sunny, thunderstorm
- give me food, give me diamonds
- heal me, jump

He can't get hurt, and he never wanders off. His voice only works on a Mac.

Fabric, for Minecraft 1.21.11 and 26.2. Needs Fabric API. Put it on the server
and in your game.

Made by Elduin, with Claude.
